Turbo NDS – NDS emulator for Android

Play the Nintendo DS games you own on your Android phone or tablet. Pick your games folder once and Turbo NDS shows every game with its own icon, remembers where you left off and keeps your saves safe.

  • Portrait and landscape layouts: stacked, side by side, big + small, one screen
  • Auto-save, 6 save slots and backups of every in-game save
  • Multi-touch buttons, Bluetooth and USB controllers
  • Fast-forward up to 4×, works offline, no ads while you play
  • No storage permission: you choose one folder

Turbo NDS includes no games, BIOS or firmware. Play only games you own. Turbo NDS is not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo.
