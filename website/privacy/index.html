Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This policy explains how the Turbo NDS Android app ("the app"), made by AppKraft Studio, handles information.

1. Summary

  • No account or login. We run no servers that receive your data.
  • Your games, saves and settings stay on your device.
  • The app shows no ads and does not use your advertising ID.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2. Your games and files

You choose folders or single files with Android's picker; the app can read only what you pick. The app reads game files there to show their names and icons and copies a game into the app's private storage to play it. Nothing is uploaded. The app does not request broad storage access.

3. Saves and settings

In-game saves, save states, play time, favourites and settings are stored only on your device. Android cloud backup and device transfer are turned off for this data. You can export your saves to a file you choose and import them again.

4. Advertising

The app currently shows no ads, loads no ad SDK features and does not access your advertising ID. If ads are added in a later version, this policy will be updated first and will explain what changes.

5. Google Play services

The app uses Google Play to show the in-app rating sheet and to check for app updates. When a newer version is available, the app asks you to install it before you continue. Google handles this under its own privacy policy; the app never learns your rating.

6. Permissions

  • Internet: for Google Play features (rating and update checks). Playing games works offline.
  • Vibration: optional touch feedback.

The app does not use your microphone (the in-game microphone button sends simulated input), camera, contacts, location permission, calls or SMS.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

8. Your choices

Clear the app's storage or uninstall the app to remove all of its data from your device. Game files in your own folders are never deleted by the app.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The latest version is always on this page with its date.
